Privacy Notice

Effective November 8, 2026 · Version 2026-11-08

Project SIKAP is the Digital Bank of Learning Resource Units of Eduardo L. Joson National High School (ELJ National High School), Division of Nueva Ecija. This notice explains what personal information the website collects from teachers and Head Teachers, why it is collected, and your rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect

  • Account details: your full name, email address and password. The password is stored securely by the sign-in service and no one at the school can read it.
  • Profile details you choose to add: phone number, location, teaching assignment, subjects, grade levels and profile photo.
  • Your work in the Digital Bank: the resources you upload and their details, file versions, review feedback, collaboration invitations, comments and proposed files, saved resources, personal notes and notifications.
  • Activity records: when a resource is created, submitted, reviewed, approved or archived, and by whom.

Why we collect it

  • To confirm you teach at the school and approve your account.
  • To credit you as the creator or contributor of every resource you work on.
  • To let Head Teachers review, approve and monitor submitted resources.
  • To notify you about your resources and collaboration requests.

Your information is not sold or used for advertising. Any information from this website used in the Project SIKAP research is reported without names.

Who can see it

  • Approved school accounts see your name on resources you create or contribute to.
  • Head Teachers see account details to approve accounts and monitor submissions.
  • Your personal notes are visible only to you.
  • The information is stored with Supabase, the database service the website runs on.

How long we keep it

[School to confirm: for example, while you teach at ELJ National High School, and up to N years after your account is closed.] Approved resources stay in the Digital Bank, with your name credited, as part of the school's permanent collection.

Your rights

Under RA 10173 you have the right to:

  • be told how your information is used, as in this notice;
  • see the personal information the website holds about you;
  • correct it, which you can do yourself on your Profile page;
  • object to its use, or ask for it to be removed or blocked;
  • get a copy of it in a common file format; and
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).
